Escrow.com is one of the most widely trusted platforms for high-value online transactions, including premium domain names. It helps protect both the buyer and the seller by ensuring that neither party needs to rely solely on trust during the transaction. In a typical escrow transaction, the buyer first submits the payment to Escrow.com. The seller is then notified to transfer the domain. Once the buyer confirms that the domain has been successfully received and ownership is complete, Escrow.com releases the payment to the seller. This process significantly reduces the risk for both parties. While direct registrar transfers are also possible when both parties agree, many buyers prefer Escrow because it provides an additional layer of security, transparency and confidence throughout the entire purchase process. The final transfer method is always decided through mutual agreement between the buyer and the seller.
