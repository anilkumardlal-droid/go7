Privacy Policy

Your Privacy Matters

At GO7.IN, your privacy is treated with respect. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit this website, how that information is used, and the measures taken to protect it.

GO7.IN is a premium domain offered for acquisition. This website is designed to provide information about the domain and allow interested buyers to submit inquiries. Only the information necessary to respond to genuine inquiries is collected, and it is never sold or shared for marketing purposes.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, you may discontinue using the website at any time.

GO7.IN is designed to provide information about a premium domain name and to allow interested buyers to contact the owner through the inquiry form. We collect only the information that you choose to provide voluntarily.

When you submit an inquiry, you may provide details such as your name, email address, subject and message. This information is used solely to understand your inquiry, respond to your questions and communicate with you regarding GO7.IN.

In addition to the information you provide directly, certain technical information may be collected automatically by your browser or device. This can include your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring pages, device information and the date and time of your visit. These standard technical logs help maintain website security, improve performance and identify potential misuse.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information, financial details or payment information through this website. Please avoid including confidential or sensitive information when submitting an inquiry unless it is necessary for the discussion.

The information you provide is used only for legitimate communication regarding GO7.IN. Typical uses include responding to inquiries, discussing purchase offers, answering questions about domain ownership, explaining the transfer process and providing additional information requested by potential buyers.

Information may also be used to improve the website, enhance security, prevent spam, detect fraudulent activity and ensure that inquiry submissions function correctly.

Your personal information is never sold, rented or shared with advertisers for marketing purposes. Access to submitted information is limited to the website owner and is used only for purposes directly related to managing inquiries concerning GO7.IN.

GO7.IN may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember certain preferences and understand how visitors interact with the website. Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser.

Most cookies used on this website are essential for normal website functionality and security. Some technologies may also help analyze general website traffic or prevent automated abuse. These cookies do not identify you personally and are not used to build advertising profiles.

You can choose to disable or remove cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of the website, including the inquiry form or security features.

GO7.IN may rely on carefully selected third-party services to provide a secure and reliable browsing experience. These services may include website hosting, content delivery, spam protection, security verification and domain-related services.

The inquiry form on this website uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help protect against spam and automated submissions. Cloudflare may process limited technical information required to perform this security check in accordance with its own privacy practices.

If a domain purchase proceeds, both parties may mutually agree to use trusted third-party platforms such as Escrow.com or accredited domain registrars to complete the transaction securely. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms of service.

GO7.IN is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites. Visitors are encouraged to review the privacy policies of any external service they choose to use.

Protecting your information is an important priority. Reasonable administrative, technical and security measures are used to help safeguard the information submitted through this website against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

Although every reasonable effort is made to maintain a secure environment, no website, server or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. For this reason, users should avoid sending highly confidential, financial or sensitive personal information through the inquiry form unless specifically requested during a mutually agreed purchase process.

Website security measures may be updated from time to time to improve protection against spam, malicious activity and unauthorized access. These improvements are implemented to provide a safer browsing experience for all visitors.

Information submitted through the inquiry form is retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage communications or complete a potential domain transaction.

If discussions do not proceed or the information is no longer required, it may be deleted from our records within a reasonable period, unless a longer retention period is required to comply with applicable legal obligations or to protect legitimate business interests.

We do not maintain personal information indefinitely without a valid business or legal reason. Our goal is to retain only the information that is genuinely necessary for communication regarding GO7.IN while respecting visitor privacy.

We believe you should have reasonable control over the personal information you choose to share with us. If you have submitted an inquiry and later wish to update, correct or request the deletion of your information, you may contact us using the details provided on this website.

Reasonable requests relating to personal information will be reviewed and handled as promptly as possible, provided they do not conflict with legal obligations, fraud prevention requirements or legitimate business interests associated with an ongoing domain transaction.

Our goal is to handle every privacy-related request fairly, responsibly and with respect for the confidentiality of everyone who contacts GO7.IN.

This website may contain links to trusted third-party websites or services for your convenience, including domain registrars, Escrow.com or security providers. Once you leave GO7.IN, the privacy practices of those websites are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them before providing personal information.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect improvements to the website, changes in applicable laws or updates to our services. Any revised version will become effective immediately after it is published on this page unless otherwise stated.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of information, please contact us using the inquiry form available on the Home page. Every genuine privacy-related inquiry is reviewed personally, and we aim to respond within 24 hours whenever reasonably possible.
