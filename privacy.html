Privacy Policy

Your privacy, security and trust are important to us.

1. About This Website

This website is operated as a central place where visitors can learn about domain names that may be available for sale and can contact us if they are interested in purchasing a particular domain.

The website may be reached through multiple domain names. Some domains may be used as the primary website address, while other domains owned or controlled by us may be configured to direct visitors here.

Because the same website can receive visitors from different domains, we use source-domain information to keep the inquiry connected with the domain the visitor originally intended to visit.

2. Domains and Website Access

Visitors may arrive at this website through another domain name that directs them here. In such cases, the visitor may still be interested in purchasing the domain they originally entered.

Arriving at this website through another domain does not change which domain the visitor may be interested in purchasing. The destination website is simply being used as the place where the inquiry and communication are handled.

To reduce confusion, the original domain can be passed to the website as source information and stored with the inquiry.

3. Information We May Collect

When you voluntarily submit an inquiry, we may collect information necessary to communicate with you and process the inquiry, such as:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • The subject and message of your inquiry.
  • The domain associated with your visit or inquiry.
  • Inquiry identification and submission date/time information.
  • Basic technical and security information such as IP address, country, city, region, device type, browser and timezone.

We do not ask you to provide unnecessary sensitive personal information in the inquiry form. Please do not submit passwords, payment-card numbers, authentication codes or other confidential credentials through the contact form.

4. How Inquiry Information Is Used

Information submitted through the inquiry form is used primarily to understand your request, identify the domain you are asking about, communicate with you, discuss availability and price, and, where applicable, proceed with a domain transaction.

Technical information may also be used for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, rate limiting and maintaining the reliability of the website.

5. Security and Anti-Spam Protection

The contact form uses security controls designed to reduce automated abuse, spam and fraudulent submissions. A security verification service may process technical information required to determine whether a submission appears to come from a genuine visitor.

Requests may also be subject to reasonable rate limits and automated checks.

6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or unrelated parties. Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the website, protect it from abuse, store inquiry records, or deliver email communications.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to a lawful request, protect the website or prevent fraud, abuse or unauthorized activity.

7. Cookies and Technical Information

The website may use essential browser storage, cookies or similar technical mechanisms where necessary for functionality, security, session management, or website operation.

Basic server-side information may also be available through hosting, security and infrastructure services. The exact information available can depend on your browser, device and the security services used to deliver the website.

8. How You Can Trust a Domain Transaction

Buying a domain name is a real transfer of a digital asset, so both the buyer and seller should take reasonable steps to make the transaction clear and verifiable.

Before paying, a buyer should:

  • Confirm the exact spelling and extension of the domain.
  • Confirm the final purchase price in writing before payment.
  • Confirm whether any additional transaction or transfer fees apply.
  • Agree on the payment method and payment recipient before sending funds.
  • Keep written records of important messages and confirmations.
  • Verify the receiving registrar or account details before transfer.
  • Do not proceed if payment instructions suddenly change without verification.
  • Never share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or unnecessary account credentials.

A legitimate domain transaction should not require you to disclose your password or one-time authentication code to another person.

9. How a Domain Sale Can Work

Every domain transaction can have slightly different circumstances, but the general process is intended to be simple and transparent.

  1. Visitor arrives: The visitor reaches the website directly or through a domain configured to direct visitors here.
  2. Inquiry is submitted: The visitor enters their name, email address and message.
  3. Domain is identified: The source domain and inquiry details are reviewed.
  4. Availability and price discussion: Availability, asking price or offer and relevant conditions are discussed.
  5. Terms are confirmed: The exact domain, final amount, payment method and transfer procedure are confirmed.
  6. Payment: Payment is made using the agreed payment method.
  7. Payment verification: Applicable payment conditions are satisfied and verified.
  8. Domain transfer: The domain transfer is initiated through the relevant registrar.
  9. Completion and records: Relevant communication and transaction records are retained.

10. Pricing and Negotiation

The value of a domain can depend on factors such as its length, memorability, extension, commercial usefulness, demand and the circumstances of the particular transaction.

An asking price, indicative price or initial offer should not be understood as a final transaction price unless the final amount is expressly confirmed.

Visitors are welcome to submit serious and reasonable offers. Once both sides agree, the final domain, purchase amount, payment method, transfer procedure and other material conditions should be recorded clearly before payment.

11. Payment and Transaction Details

Payment is handled only after the important transaction details have been discussed and agreed.

Before sending money, the buyer should have written confirmation of the exact domain name, final agreed price, payment recipient, payment method and basic domain-transfer procedure.

If payment instructions suddenly change, do not assume the new information is genuine. Pause and request confirmation through a trusted communication channel.

12. Secure Escrow or Third-Party Transaction Services

Where mutually agreed or appropriate, a recognized third-party escrow or transaction service may be used. Its own terms, privacy policy, fees and verification requirements will apply to that part of the transaction.

13. Domain Transfer

After the agreed payment and transaction conditions have been satisfied, the domain is transferred using the normal procedure supported by the relevant registrar and registry.

The buyer is responsible for providing accurate receiving details and completing any actions that the registrar requires.

14. Transaction Records

Relevant emails, inquiry information, payment confirmations and transaction communications may be retained for legitimate business, accounting, dispute resolution, security or legal purposes, subject to applicable requirements.

15. Fraud Prevention

Please be cautious of anyone asking you to pay to an unexpected account, requesting passwords or authentication codes, changing payment details at the last moment, or creating unusual urgency.

If payment instructions appear different from previously confirmed details, verify them through a trusted communication channel before sending funds.

16. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect inquiry information against unauthorized access, misuse or loss. However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

17. Data Retention

Inquiry and transaction-related information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for communication, business records, security, dispute handling, accounting or legal obligations.

18. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with an inquiry, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy-related concern. Requests may need to be reasonably verified before information is disclosed or changed.

19. Third-Party Services

Website hosting, security, email delivery, database, domain registration, payment or escrow processes may involve third-party providers.

For example, security verification may be provided by Cloudflare and email delivery may involve an email service provider. Their processing is governed by their applicable policies and terms.

20. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, domain-sale process, security practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version published on this page will apply to future interactions after it is posted.

21. Questions About Privacy or a Domain Transaction

If you are not sure which domain your inquiry relates to, how visitors may arrive through another domain, how your information is being used, how the price was determined, which payment method is being proposed, or how the transfer will take place, please ask before proceeding.

For a domain purchase, it is especially important that the exact domain name, final amount, payment recipient and transfer process are clear before money is sent.
