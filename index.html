Frequently asked questions

Questions & Answers

Can I buy a domain that is already registered?

Yes. Many premium domains are already owned by individuals or businesses. If the owner is open to selling, I can help identify acquisition opportunities and assist throughout the process. While availability can never be guaranteed, many previously registered domains do become available through negotiation.

Do you own every domain listed on this website?

Most domains offered are from my own portfolio. For domains not owned by me, I may be able to assist with acquisition opportunities when the current owner is willing to sell.

Is Escrow available?

Yes. For eligible premium domain transactions, secure Escrow services can be used whenever both buyer and seller agree. This provides additional protection for both parties.

How long does a domain transfer take?

The transfer time depends on the registrar and the transaction method. In many cases, transfers are completed within a few hours, while some registrar-to-registrar transfers may take several days.

Can you help me find the perfect domain for my business?

Absolutely. Whether you're launching a startup, rebranding an existing business or looking for a memorable premium domain, I can recommend suitable domains from my portfolio and help explore acquisition opportunities for domains already registered by others.
