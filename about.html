1 About GO7.IN

GO7.IN is a domain-focused website created to provide visitors with a simple and professional way to explore domain names that may be available for acquisition.

Instead of maintaining separate websites for every domain, multiple domains may direct visitors to GO7.IN. This allows domain-related inquiries and communication to be handled from one central platform.

The purpose is straightforward: identify the domain, understand the available information, contact us, discuss the terms and, when both parties agree, complete the transaction through an appropriate domain-transfer process.

2 What We Do

Our primary focus is the ownership, presentation and potential sale of domain names. A domain can be an important digital asset for a business, project, brand, product or personal venture.

GO7.IN provides a communication point where interested buyers can submit an inquiry about a particular domain and begin a conversation about availability, pricing and transaction terms.

Our simple approach: Domain → Inquiry → Discussion → Agreement → Payment → Transfer

3 Our Domain Portfolio

Domains presented through GO7.IN may differ in length, extension, naming style, commercial potential and intended use. Some may be short brandable names, while others may be suitable for specific businesses, projects or online services.

Domain availability and pricing can change. A domain shown or discussed at one point should not automatically be considered available until its availability and final transaction terms have been confirmed.

4 Finding the Right Domain

Choosing a domain is an important decision. A good domain should be easy to remember, easy to communicate and appropriate for the intended business, project or brand.

Visitors can contact us about a domain they are interested in. The inquiry can then be reviewed so that the exact domain, requirements and expectations are clear before any transaction is discussed further.

5 Domain Inquiries

A domain inquiry is the starting point for communication between an interested buyer and us. Visitors can provide their name, email address and message so that we can understand what they are looking for.

When a visitor reaches GO7.IN through another domain, the source domain may also be associated with the inquiry so that the correct domain remains clear throughout the conversation.

6 Pricing and Negotiation

Domain pricing can vary depending on factors such as the domain name, extension, length, memorability, commercial usefulness, demand and other circumstances.

An asking price or an initial offer should not be considered a final transaction price until the final terms have been clearly agreed between the parties.

Serious and reasonable offers may be considered. Once an agreement is reached, the exact domain, final amount, payment method and transfer procedure should be confirmed clearly before payment.

7 Transparent Communication

We believe that important transaction details should be clear before a buyer sends money. The exact domain name, agreed price, payment recipient and transfer process should be understood by both sides.

If any important detail changes during the conversation, especially payment instructions or transfer information, the change should be independently verified before proceeding.

8 Secure Domain Transactions

Domain transactions involve the transfer of a digital asset. Buyers and sellers should therefore take reasonable precautions before completing a transaction.

Important details should be confirmed in writing and relevant communication should be retained until the transaction has been completed.

  • Confirm the exact spelling and extension of the domain.
  • Confirm the final purchase price in writing.
  • Confirm the payment recipient before sending funds.
  • Understand the domain-transfer procedure.
  • Keep important communication and confirmations.
  • Verify receiving registrar or account details.
  • Verify unexpected changes to payment instructions.
  • Never share passwords or authentication codes.
Important security rule: Never provide your password, OTP, recovery code or other authentication credentials to another person as part of a domain transaction.

9 How a Domain Sale Can Work

Every transaction can have different circumstances, but a normal domain sale can follow a straightforward process.

Step 1 — Domain is selected: The visitor identifies the domain they are interested in.
Step 2 — Inquiry is submitted: The visitor sends their name, email address and message through the inquiry form.
Step 3 — Domain is confirmed: The exact domain associated with the inquiry is identified.
Step 4 — Price is discussed: Availability, asking price or an offer can be discussed.
Step 5 — Terms are confirmed: The exact domain, final amount, payment method and transfer procedure are agreed.
Step 6 — Payment: The buyer makes payment using the agreed method.
Step 7 — Payment verification: Applicable payment conditions are checked before the transfer is completed.
Step 8 — Domain transfer: The domain is transferred through the applicable registrar process.
Step 9 — Completion: Relevant transaction records and communication are retained.

10 Payment and Transaction Details

Payment arrangements are discussed and agreed before funds are sent. The appropriate payment method may depend on the specific transaction and the circumstances of the buyer and seller.

Buyers should confirm the exact domain, final amount, payment recipient and transfer procedure before making payment.

If payment instructions suddenly change, the buyer should pause the transaction and verify the new information through a trusted communication channel.

11 Domain Transfer

Once the applicable transaction conditions have been satisfied, the domain transfer can proceed through the relevant registrar or standard domain-transfer process.

Transfer requirements can vary depending on the domain extension, registrar and account setup.

Buyers should provide accurate receiving-account information and complete any authorization steps required by the registrar.

12 Third-Party Services

Depending on the transaction, domain registration, payment, escrow, email, hosting or security services may involve third-party providers.

When a third-party service is used, its own terms, policies, verification requirements and fees may apply to that part of the transaction.

13 Security and Fraud Prevention

We encourage visitors to remain cautious when dealing with online transactions. Unexpected payment instructions, unusual urgency, requests for passwords or authentication codes and unexplained changes should always be treated carefully.

Important transaction information should be verified before sending funds or transferring a domain.

We do not believe important transaction details should depend on assumptions. Clear written communication helps prevent misunderstandings for everyone involved.
