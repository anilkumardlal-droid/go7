3 A Professional & Transparent Approach

I believe a domain transaction should be clear for both the buyer and the seller. Important details should be discussed and understood before any payment or transfer takes place.

This includes confirming the exact domain name, agreed purchase price, payment method and domain transfer procedure.

Buyers are encouraged to ask questions and verify important transaction details before proceeding. There should be no need to share passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or other confidential credentials during a normal domain transaction.

4 How I Handle Domain Transactions

Step 1 — Inquiry: The buyer contacts me regarding a specific domain through the website or email.
Step 2 — Discussion: We discuss the domain, the buyer’s requirements, availability and the proposed price or offer.
Step 3 — Terms: Before payment, the exact domain, final price, payment method and transfer procedure are clearly established.
Step 4 — Payment: Payment is made using the mutually agreed payment method for the particular transaction.
Step 5 — Transfer: Once the agreed transaction conditions have been satisfied, the domain transfer is initiated using the applicable registrar process.
Step 6 — Completion: Relevant communication and transaction records can be retained by both parties for their records.

5 For Buyers

If you are considering purchasing a domain from GO7.IN, you are welcome to ask questions before making any commitment.

I encourage buyers to independently verify the exact domain name, agreed price, payment instructions and transfer details before sending funds.

My principle: Clear communication first, agreement second, payment and transfer only after the important details are understood.
