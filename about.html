My Approach to Domain Names

My approach to domain investing is based on simplicity, quality, and long-term potential.

I prefer domain names that can communicate efficiently. Long or complicated names can sometimes create unnecessary friction when a customer has to remember or type them.

A concise domain can provide a cleaner foundation for branding. I also consider how a name sounds when spoken because domains are frequently shared through conversations, meetings, advertisements, social media, and networking.

Most importantly, I try to view a domain from the perspective of the person who may eventually use it. A domain is not valuable merely because it is available or short. Its real potential comes from what a buyer can build around it.
