My Approach to Domain Names

My approach to domain investing is based on simplicity, quality, and long-term potential.

I prefer domain names that are easy to remember, pronounce, communicate, and use as part of a brand. I also consider how a domain sounds when spoken, because domain names are often shared through conversations, meetings, advertising, social media, and networking. Most importantly, I try to view each domain from the perspective of the person who may eventually use it. A domain is not valuable simply because it is short or available. Its potential comes from what a buyer can build around it.

What Makes a Domain Valuable

There is no single formula for determining whether a domain is right for a particular business. Some of the qualities I consider include:

  • Short and memorable structure
  • Easy pronunciation and spelling
  • Strong brandability
  • Relevant domain extension
  • Flexibility for different business models
  • Clear digital identity potential
  • Ease of communicating the domain

Ultimately, every buyer should evaluate a domain according to their own requirements and objectives.

Why Short Domains Matter

Short domains can offer a natural advantage in simplicity. They generally require fewer characters and can be easier to remember, communicate, and type. However, being short alone does not make a domain valuable. Meaning, brandability, extension, usability, and market demand also matter. GO7.IN is interesting to me because its compact structure provides flexibility while allowing the future owner to create their own meaning and identity around the name.

The .IN Domain Extension

The .IN extension is India's country-code top-level domain. It provides a clear connection with India and can be a natural choice for businesses, organizations, entrepreneurs, and projects targeting the Indian market. For me, GO7.IN combines the compact nature of a short domain with the identity of the .IN extension.
