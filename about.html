Hello, I’m Anil Srivastav. I’m an independent domain investor and seller, and I have been working with domain names for more than six years.

Over the years, I have built and managed a portfolio of domain names that I believe have potential as digital identities for businesses, startups, entrepreneurs, creators, and online projects.

My Domain Portfolio

My interest is mainly in domain names that are short, easy to remember, simple to communicate, and have the potential to become a useful part of a brand.

Some domains are short and brandable, while others may have a particular word, meaning, extension, or use case that makes them interesting. I don’t believe there is one formula that makes every domain valuable. What matters in the end is how well a name fits the person, business, or project that wants to use it.

That is why I try to look at domains from both sides — as a domain investor and from the perspective of someone who may actually build something around the name.

How GO7.IN Fits In

GO7.IN is the central website I use to present and manage this part of my domain portfolio. It is not limited to a single domain name.

I own or manage different domain names, and some of those domains may be configured to redirect visitors to GO7.IN. This allows interested visitors to reach one common website instead of having to find a separate website for every domain.

My Experience in the Domain Industry

I have been involved with domain names for more than six years. During this time, I have learned that choosing a good domain is not simply about finding the shortest name available.

Memorability, pronunciation, simplicity, brand potential, extension relevance, usability, and the audience a name could serve all play a part.

Every buyer has different goals, so a domain that makes sense for one business may not necessarily be the right choice for another.

What I Look for in a Domain

When I evaluate a domain, I generally look for qualities such as:

  • Short and easy-to-remember structure
  • Simple pronunciation and spelling
  • Strong brand potential
  • A relevant and useful domain extension
  • Flexibility for different types of projects
  • Ease of communicating the name
  • Potential to become part of a clear digital identity

These are simply the qualities I look for. Ultimately, the right domain is the one that fits the buyer’s own plans and objectives.

Why Short Domains Matter

Short domains can be easier to remember, type, say, and share. They can also give a brand a clean and simple identity.

But being short by itself does not make a domain valuable. Meaning, usability, brandability, the extension, and market demand can all matter as well.

The .IN Domain Extension

The .IN extension is India’s country-code top-level domain. It can be a natural choice for businesses, entrepreneurs, organizations, and projects that want a clear connection with India.

I like the combination of a concise domain name with an extension that has a clear Indian identity.

A Domain Is More Than a Web Address

A domain name can become more than an address entered into a browser. It can become the name customers remember, the address used in marketing, and an important part of a company or project’s online identity.

For a startup, entrepreneur, creator, or established business, the right name can become something they continue to use for many years.

How I Work With Buyers
